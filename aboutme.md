## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Single-molecule biophysics** — Investigating the behavior of individual biomolecules to uncover mechanisms hidden in ensemble averages
- **Membrane protein dynamics** — Understanding how membrane proteins function, move, and interact within lipid environments
- **Protein-membrane interactions** — Exploring the physical principles governing how proteins engage with cellular membranes
- **Emergent phenomena in biological systems** — Studying how molecular-scale interactions give rise to complex, collective behaviors
