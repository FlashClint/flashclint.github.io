## 🔬 Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding the physical principles that govern molecular-scale biological processes. I work at the intersection of **single-molecule biophysics**, **membrane protein dynamics**, and **protein-membrane interactions**, using a combination of experimental and computational approaches.

**Affiliation**: [Prof. Fang Jiao's Lab](https://english.iop.cas.cn/pe/faculty/?id=4432), [Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://english.iop.cas.cn/)

---

## 🧪 Research Directions

### 1. Pore-Forming Proteins and Membrane Interactions
Understanding how pore-forming proteins (e.g., NINJ1, gasdermins) interact with and remodel lipid membranes. Using high-speed AFM (HS-AFM) and fluorescence-based leakage assays to capture the real-time dynamics of membrane disruption, pore formation, and protein oligomerization at the nanoscale.

**Key publication**: *Dynamic dissection of NINJ1-mediated plasma membrane rupture reveals a multi-stage process*. Jiao, F. et al. Nature Structural & Molecular Biology (under review). [Research Square Preprint](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-5142979/v1)

### 2. Peptide Self-Assembly
Investigating how peptides and small proteins self-assemble into higher-order structures on membrane surfaces. Exploring the biophysical principles governing peptide aggregation, amyloid formation, and their interactions with lipid bilayers.

**Key publication**: TBD — [ACS Nano](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsnano.5c14731)

### 3. Mechanisms of Antibiotic Action
Exploring how antibiotics and antimicrobial peptides interact with bacterial membranes, leading to membrane permeabilization and cell death. Combining single-molecule techniques with biochemical assays to elucidate molecular mechanisms.

*This direction is currently being developed.*

## 🔧 Techniques & Methods

- **High-speed atomic force microscopy (HS-AFM)** — Real-time imaging of membrane proteins dynamics
- **Single-molecule fluorescence microscopy**
- **Total internal reflection fluorescence (TIRF) microscopy**
- **Calcein-containing liposome leakage assay** — Membrane permeability measurements
- **Protein purification and reconstitution into liposomes**
- **Lipid membrane model systems** (supported lipid bilayers, GUVs, proteoliposomes)
- **Data analysis and image processing**
